I'm **Senzo Sigcu** — a Postgraduate Diploma candidate in Cybersecurity (NQF 8) at Nelson Mandela University, building on a foundation in software development. My work runs along two tracks that usually stay apart: **security governance and risk** (NIST CSF, ISO 27001, COBIT) and **hands-on offensive security** (ethical hacking and application security). This site is where I document what I test in the lab, what evidence the attack leaves behind, and how I'd advise fixing it.

Every write-up follows the same spine — enumerate, identify, exploit, then switch to the defender's chair: what a blue team would see, and which control would have stopped it. The point isn't to collect trophies; it's to show I can move between the attacker's keyboard and the audit checklist without dropping either.

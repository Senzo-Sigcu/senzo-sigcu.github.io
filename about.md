About

I'm Senzo Sigcu. I came into cybersecurity through software development, and that developer's background is deliberate: I'd rather understand how systems are actually built before I set out to test or defend them.

My focus is hands-on technical security. On the offensive side: ethical hacking and application security, working with Nmap, Burp Suite, Greenbone/GVM and Metasploit through a full penetration-testing methodology. On the defensive side: reading each attack back from the logs it leaves and identifying the control or alert that would stop it. Understanding both sides of the same event, the attacker's actions and the defender's evidence, is what I am building toward.

My postgraduate research examines anthropomorphic AI and the CASA paradigm: how trust transfer to human-like AI can erode organisational cybersecurity culture, particularly among Gen-Z users.

Each write-up documents real lab work: the commands I ran, the output they produced, and the reasoning behind each decision.

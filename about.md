I'm Senzo Sigcu, a Postgraduate Diploma candidate in Cybersecurity (NQF 8) at Nelson Mandela University, building on a foundation in software development. My earlier NMU qualifications were in IT and software development. That developer's background is deliberate: I'd rather understand how systems are actually built before I set out to test or govern them.

My work spans two sides that most people keep separate. On the **governance and risk** side: security frameworks (NIST CSF, ISO 27001, COBIT) along with risk management and threat modelling. On the **offensive** side: ethical hacking and application security, working hands-on with Nmap, Burp Suite, Greenbone/GVM and Metasploit through a full penetration-testing methodology. The thread connecting them is simple: I want to be able to both test a control *and* judge whether it belongs in a risk register.

My postgraduate research examines anthropomorphic AI and the CASA paradigm: how trust transfer to human-like AI can erode organisational cybersecurity culture, particularly among Gen-Z users.

Alongside my studies I work as a Tech Buddy at the NMU Business School, supporting the virtual learning infrastructure for its executive education programmes.

This site is the evidence, not the claim. Each write-up is meant to show the reasoning, not just the result.
